# Privacy Policy — Women Impact Club

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Women Impact Consulting W.L.L. ("Women Impact Club", "we", "us") operates the Women Impact Club mobile app and the website womenimpactclub.com. This policy explains what we collect and why.

## Information we collect

**Account and membership information.** If you join the club or sign in to the members area, we collect the details you provide: name, email address, phone number, and any profile information you choose to add (company, role, bio, links).

**Event registration information.** When you register for an event, we record which event you registered for. Registration may continue over WhatsApp; messages you send there are handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy policy.

**Notification data.** If you enable event reminders, reminders are scheduled and stored on your device. If push notifications are enabled, a device push token is stored so we can send event announcements. The token identifies the device, not you personally.

**Technical data.** Standard server logs (IP address, device type, timestamps) generated when the app loads content from our website.

We do not collect location data, contacts, photos, or health data. We do not use third-party advertising networks and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## How we use information
- To provide and manage your membership and account.
- To register you for events and confirm your place.
- To send event reminders and club announcements you have opted into.
- To keep the app and website secure and to fix problems.

## Sharing
We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the club — hosting and database providers, and Apple/Google push notification services — and only as needed to deliver the service, or where required by law.

## Retention
We keep membership and event data for as long as your membership is active and for as long as needed afterwards to meet legal and accounting obligations. Push tokens are deleted when notifications are disabled or the app is uninstalled.

## Your choices and rights
- Turn off reminders and push notifications at any time in your device settings.
- Request a copy of your data, correction, or deletion of your account by contacting us.
- Uninstalling the app removes locally stored reminders.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes
We may update this policy; the "last updated" date above will change and material updates will be announced in the app or by email.
