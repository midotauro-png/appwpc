## Description [4000]
Women Impact Club is the mobile home of a growing community of 500+ women founders, professionals, experts and entrepreneurs across Bahrain and the GCC.

The app gives you everything the club offers, in your pocket:

UPCOMING EVENTS
Browse workshops, forums and the annual Women Impact Summit in a fast native events list — dates, times, venues, speakers, member and non-member pricing, and how many spots are left.

EVENT REMINDERS
Tap "Remind me" on any event and the app schedules a notification before it starts, so you never miss a session you wanted to attend. Reminders live on your device and can be turned off at any time.

ONE-TAP REGISTRATION
Register for an event in seconds — no long forms. Registration opens a prefilled message so the team can confirm your seat straight away.

PRIVATE MEMBERS NETWORK
Access the members area to view curated profiles of women founders, professionals and experts, and find collaborators, clients and partners inside the Women Impact ecosystem.

YOUR ACCOUNT
Sign in to manage your membership, view your registrations and keep your profile up to date.

Women Impact Club is a space where women grow, connect and get visible. Stop building alone — join a community designed to help you expand your network, grow your business and access real opportunities.

Membership is required for some areas of the app. Learn more at womenimpactclub.com.
